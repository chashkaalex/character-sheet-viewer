Character Sheet Viewer

Welcome! Enter your Google Docs document ID below to view your character sheet.

How to get your Document ID:

1. Open your character sheet in Google Docs

2. Look at the URL in your browser's address bar

3. Copy the long string of letters and numbers between "/d/" and "/edit"

4. Paste that ID into the field above and click "Get"
